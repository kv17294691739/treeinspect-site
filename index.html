Tree Inspect

A field app for arborists and surveyors who record trees on site.

App Store – coming soon Google Play – coming soon

Tree Inspect lets you build a custom survey form for each job, pin each tree on a site plan, attach marked-up photos and capture GPS. It works offline, and when the survey is finished you can export selected trees to Excel or a photo zip.

What it does

  • Custom fields: text, numbers, dropdowns, checklists, dates and GPS
  • Species catalogue and reusable survey templates
  • Site-plan map with pins, groups and shapes
  • Photos with captions and annotations
  • Search, filter and duplicate trees
  • Quoting, including schedule of rates (Pro)
  • Excel, photo and full project export
  • Works on the phone without a signal; your survey data stays on your device

User guides and templates

New to Tree Inspect? The user guides walk through every screen with screenshots: projects, tree forms, maps and pins, photos, GPS, templates, quoting, export and backups. You can also download sample Excel templates for project fields, SOR price lists, species and whole projects.
