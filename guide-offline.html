Working offline

What works without a signal, and what needs the internet.

Tree Inspect is built for sites with poor or no signal. Your projects, trees, photos, maps and templates are stored on the phone, so you can survey without a connection.

Works offlineNeeds the internet
  • Creating and editing projects and trees
  • Photos, captions and markup
  • Site plans, pins and drawings
  • GPS location
  • Templates, species catalogue and SOR lists
  • Creating export files and backups
  • Buying, restoring or checking a Pro subscription
  • Adverts and the ad consent form (Free version)
  • Sending an export by email or uploading it to cloud storage

Tips

  • Before going to site, attach the site plan and check the template on Wi-Fi.
  • If your Pro period ends while you are offline, Pro may continue for a short grace period (currently 14 days). Connect again so the store can confirm your subscription.
  • Make a Full export when you are back in signal, and keep a copy off the phone.
